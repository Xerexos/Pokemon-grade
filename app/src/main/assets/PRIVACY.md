PokéGrade Privacy Policy
Version 1 · Effective 24 November 2026

PokéGrade is a private app shared between a small group of friends. It is run by the person who gave you the app (the "app owner"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. You must accept it before using the app.

1. WHAT STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

These stay on your phone and are not sent anywhere by the app:
• Your camera photos. They are analysed on your phone: the grade, the text reading and most authenticity checks run on the device.
• Your settings, your Anthropic API key (if you add one) and your saved "reference back".
The app never reads your other photos, contacts, location or any other personal data. The gallery picker only gives the app the single photo you choose.

2. WHAT IS SENT TO OTHER SERVICES

• Card lookup (TCGdex, api.tcgdex.net). The card name, number and HP read from the photo are sent to look up the card and its prices. No photo is sent.
• AI second opinion (Anthropic). Only if you add your own Anthropic API key and tap "Ask Claude" (or turn on automatic AI checks). The card photos are then sent to Anthropic under your own Anthropic account and Anthropic's terms.

3. THE SHARED DATASET (OPTIONAL)

You can choose to share labelled scans to help improve the app's grading and fake detection. This is off unless you turn it on, on the consent screen or later in Settings.

When it is on, a scan is uploaded only after you add a label (for example "graded slab, PSA cert 12345678", "I pulled this from a pack" or "confirmed fake") and tap Upload. An upload contains:
• the cropped photos of the card's front and, if scanned, its back;
• the app's measurements (grade, centering, wear values, colour measurements) and the card it matched;
• the label you chose, including any grading-company cert number you typed;
• a random anonymous ID created for your install, the upload time and the app version.

It does NOT contain your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or device identifiers. Don't photograph anything other than the card (such as people, documents or addresses).

Where it is stored: Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), in a project owned by the app owner. Google processes the data under its Firebase terms.

Who can see it: only the app owner, who uses it to test and improve the app's grading and fake detection. It is never sold or used for advertising. Grading-company cert numbers may be checked against that company's public lookup (for example PSA's cert verification) to confirm the grade.

4. KEEPING AND DELETING YOUR DATA

Uploaded scans are kept until they are no longer useful for improving the app, or until you ask for them to be deleted.
• In Settings, "Delete my uploaded scans" removes everything uploaded from your install.
• If you uninstall the app first, your anonymous ID is lost. Before uninstalling, either delete your scans or note the ID shown in Settings, then ask the app owner to remove them.
• You can turn sharing off at any time in Settings. Nothing more is uploaded after that.

5. CHILDREN

The app is not intended for children under 13. Don't share scans if you are under 13.

6. CHANGES

If this policy changes, the app will show the new version and ask you to accept it again before you continue.

7. CONTACT

Questions or deletion requests: contact the app owner, the person who shared PokéGrade with you.
